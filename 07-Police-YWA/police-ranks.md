#### 1. Police Constable (PC) – BPS-07

Responsibilities: Chowk, road, police station, ya patrol par duty. Crime report lena, basic investigation assist karna, public se deal karna, traffic control, escort etc.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Head Constable (HC) aur Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) ke under.

#### 2. Head Constable (HC) – BPS-09

Responsibilities: Constables ko supervise karta hai, duty assign karta hai, chhoti investigation aur station ka day-to-day kaam dekhta hai.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: ASI aur Sub-Inspector (SI) ke under.

#### 3. Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) – BPS-11

Responsibilities: Investigation assist karta hai, FIR registration mein help, patrol teams lead karta hai, chhoti cases handle karta hai.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Sub-Inspector (SI) aur Inspector ke under.

#### 4. Sub-Inspector (SI) – BPS-14

Responsibilities: Investigation officer hota hai, FIR register karta hai, cases investigate karta hai, police station ka operational kaam chalata hai. Aksar SHO (Station House Officer) bhi banta hai.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Inspector aur DSP/ASP ke under.

#### 5. Inspector – BPS-16

Responsibilities: Police station ka overall in-charge (SHO), investigation supervise, staff manage, law & order maintain. Bade cases dekhna.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: DSP ya ASP (SDPO) ke under.

#### 6. Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) – BPS-17

Responsibilities: PSP (Police Service of Pakistan) officers hote hain. Sub-division (tehsil level) ka charge, operations, investigation supervise, training.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Superintendent of Police (SP) ke under.

#### 7. Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) – BPS-17

Responsibilities: Provincial cadre officers. Sub-division (SDPO) ka charge, police stations supervise, law & order, investigation monitor.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Superintendent of Police (SP) ke under.

#### 8. Superintendent of Police (SP) – BPS-18

Responsibilities: District ya specific unit ka in-charge (DPO – District Police Officer). Poora district police manage, crime control, administration.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) ke under.

#### 9. Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) – BPS-19

Responsibilities: Bade districts, ranges, ya specialized units (jaise CTD, Special Branch) ka head. Multiple SPs ko control karta hai.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Deputy Inspector General (DIG) ke under.

#### 10. Deputy Inspector General (DIG) – BPS-20

Responsibilities: Region / Range / Zone ka in-charge (RPO). Multiple districts supervise, policy implementation, major operations.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Additional Inspector General (Addl. IG) ke under.

#### 11. Additional Inspector General (Addl. IG) – BPS-21

Responsibilities: Province ke major ranges (jaise Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur) ya specialized wings ka head. DIG’s ko control, high-level policy.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Inspector General of Police (IGP) ke under.

#### 12. Inspector General of Police (IGP) – BPS-22

Responsibilities: Poori Sindh Police ka top boss. Policy banata hai, overall command, government se coordination, major decisions.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Sindh Government (Chief Minister / Home Department) ke under. Abhi Javed Alam Odho hain

## Sindh Prison & Corrections Service Police Department (SPCS) Ranks

#### 1. Prison Constable (PC) – BPS-05

* Responsibilities: Jail ke andar security, prisoners ko watch karna, gates, barracks aur compounds ki hifazat, basic duties (escort, counting etc.).
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Senior Prison Constable aur Head Constable ke under.

#### 2. Senior Prison Constable (Sr. PC) – BPS-05

* Responsibilities: Normal Constable se thoda senior. Team lead karta hai, duty assign karta hai, chhoti supervision.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Head Constable (HC) ke under.

#### 3. Head Constable Prison (HC) – BPS-07

* Responsibilities: Constables ko control karta hai, shift duty manage karta hai, barracks ki daily checking, discipline maintain.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) ke under.

#### 4. Assistant Sub-Inspector Prison (ASI) – BPS-09

* Responsibilities: Section/ward ka in-charge, prisoners ki counting, security rounds, staff supervision, chhoti inquiries.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Sub-Inspector (SI) ke under.

#### 5. Sub-Inspector (SI) – BPS-14

Responsibilities: Jail ke ek major section/block ka charge, security operations, staff management, prisoners ke movement control.
Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Assistant Superintendent ke under.

#### 6. Assistant Superintendent – BPS-16

* Responsibilities: Jail ke daily administration mein help, security, discipline, prisoners ke records, staff supervision. Aksar jail ke different wings ka charge.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Deputy Superintendent ke under.

#### 7. Deputy Superintendent – BPS-17

* Responsibilities: Jail ke overall operations mein Superintendent ki help, security, discipline, prisoners welfare, staff control. Chhoti jails mein Officer In-charge bhi ho sakta hai.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Superintendent Prison ke under.

#### 8. Superintendent Prison (Supdt.) – BPS-18

* Responsibilities: Ek jail (District ya Central Jail) ka Officer In-charge. Poori jail ki security, administration, prisoners ki hifazat, discipline, welfare aur daily management ki zimmedari.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Senior Superintendent ya DIG ke under.

#### 9. Senior Superintendent Prison (Senior Supdt.) – BPS-19

* Responsibilities: Badi Central Jails ya multiple jails ka charge. Higher level administration, inspection, policy implementation.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Deputy Inspector General (DIG) ke under.

#### 10. Deputy Inspector General of Prisons (DIG) – BPS-20

* Responsibilities: Region/Zone ka head (jaise Karachi, Hyderabad, Sukkur Region). Multiple jails supervise karta hai, inspections, policy follow-up, major issues handle.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Inspector General of Prisons (IG) ke under.

#### 11. Inspector General of Prisons (IG) – BPS-21

* Responsibilities: Poori Sindh Prisons Department ka top boss. Policy banana, overall control, government se coordination, reforms, security standards, prisoners welfare.
* Kis ke under kaam karta hai: Sindh Government (Chief Minister / Home Department / Prisons Minister) ke under.
* Current IG: Fida Hussain Mastoi (October 2026 tak).
