



"Has" aur "Have" ka istemal English me present perfect tense aur possession (milkiyat) dikhane ke liye hota hai. Neeche Roman Urdu me asaan tareeke se samjhaya gaya hai:

### Have Meaning:

#### 1. Lijiye / Le lijiye**
* Jab kisi ko kuch diya jata hai

1. Have some tea.
* (Thodi chai lijiye.)

2. Have a seat.
* (Baith jaiye.)

#### 2. Kisi cheez ka paas hona (Milkiyat)
* Jab kisi ke paas koi cheez ho:

1. I have a pen.
* (Mere paas ek pen hai.)

2. They have a car.
* (Unke paas ek gari hai.)

#### 3. Kuch kar lena / Experience hona
* Jab kisi ne kuch khaya, piya, ya experience kiya ho:
1. I have lunch at 2 PM.
* (Main do baje lunch karta hoon.)

2. Have fun!
* (Maze karein!)


###  "Have" ka istemal:
**Subjects ke sath:**

1. I
2. You
3. We
4. They
5. Aur sab plural nouns

### "Has" ka istemal:
**Subjects ke sath:**
1. He
2. She
3. It
5. Aur sab singular nouns
