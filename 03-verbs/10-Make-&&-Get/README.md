

### Make as a cositive verb
“Kisi se koi kaam karwana” ya “kisi ko kisi kaam par majboor karna.”

**Subject + make/makes + object (pronoun) + verb (1st form)**


| **Subject Pronoun** | **Object Pronoun** | **Urdu Meaning**       |
| ------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------- |
| I                   | me                 | میں → مجھے             |
| You                 | you                | تم / آپ → تمہیں / آپکو |
| He                  | him                | وہ (لڑکا) → اسے        |
| She                 | her                | وہ (لڑکی) → اسے        |
| It                  | it                 | یہ → اسے               |
| We                  | us                 | ہم → ہمیں              |
| They                | them               | وہ سب → انہیں          |


1. I made him cry.
Maine usko rula diya.

2. She makes me laugh.
Woh mujhe hansati hai.

3. They made us clean the room.
Unho ne humse kamra saaf karwaya.
