
### پر Different uses:
| Preposition | Meaning / Use                                                | Roman Urdu Explanation                                                | Example                          | Urdu Translation                  |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **on**      | Kisi surface par (touch mein)                                | Kisi cheez ke **upar**, jo kisi surface ko touch kar rahi ho          | The book is on the table.        | Kitaab mez **par** hai.           |
| **upon**    | Formal/poetic "on", ya kisi action ke turant baad            | **Shaista ya kahani wali** English mein, ya kisi cheez par achanak se | He jumped upon the horse.        | Woh ghoday **par** chadh gaya.    |
| **onto**    | Kisi surface **par move karna** (action ke sath)             | Kisi cheez **par chadhna** ya us par move hona                        | The cat jumped onto the table.   | Billi mez **par chalang** lagayi. |
| **at**      | Specific jagah, point, ya waqt                               | **Mukhsus jagah ya waqt** par                                         | He is at the door.               | Woh darwazay **par** hai.         |
| **above**   | Kisi cheez se upar (touch nahi kar rahi)                     | Kisi cheez **se oonchai par**, lekin contact nahi                     | The fan is above the table.      | Fan mez **se upar** hai.          |
| **over**    | Kisi cheez ke upar, kabhi kabhi covering ya movement ke liye | **Upar se cover** karna ya kisi cheez ke **upar** hona                | He put a blanket over the child. | Usne bache **par** kambal dala.   |


## 1. ON

### 1. Surface (kisi cheez ke upar)
* Jab koi cheez kisi aur cheez ke upar ho, touch karte hue.
* Jab koi cheez kisi surface par ho.

1. The book is on the table.
(Kitaab table ke upar hai)

2. The phone is on the bed.
(Phone bistar ke upar hai)


### 2. Days and Dates & Festival
* Specific din ya tareek ke liye. 
* Kisi specific din ya tareekh ko refer karne ke liye.

1. I will come on Monday.
(Main Monday ko aaoon ga)

2. Her birthday is on 12th July.
(Us ka birthday 12 July ko hai)

3. i go home on eid


### 3. Devices (screens, tools, media)
* Jab hum mobile, computer, TV ya kisi app ka use kar rahe hoon.
* Jab kisi device ya platform ka zikr ho.

1. I saw it on TV.
(Maine ye TV par dekha)

2. She is talking on the phone.
(Woh phone par baat kar rahi hai)

3. I read the article on the website.
(Main ne article website par padha)

### 4. Transport (Public or shared transport)
* Jab kisi bus, train, plane ya bike ka zikr ho.
* Jab kisi vehicle ya ride ka use ho raha ho.

1. He is on the bus.
(Woh bus mein hai)

2. I came on a bike.
(Main bike par aaya)

3. They are on a flight.
(Woh flight mein hain)


### 5. About a topic (Kisi mawzu par)
* Jab kisi topic, subject ya matter ka zikr ho.
* Kisi topic par baat karna.

1. He gave a speech on climate change.
(Usne climate change par speech di)

2. I’m working on a project.
(Main ek project par kaam kar raha hoon)


### 6. Dependence / Condition (Base hone ki surat me)
*  Jab kuch kisi cheez par depend kare.
* Jab koi cheez kisi aur cheez par depend kare.

1. It depends on you.
(Ye tum par depend karta hai)

2. We rely on him.
(Hum us par bharosa karte hain)


### 7. Position in direction (upar se chippka ya side)

1. There is a mark on the wall.
(Deewar par nishaan hai)

2. There’s a painting on the ceiling.
(Chhat par painting hai)

3. the book is on the table


## 2. AT
* "At" kisi specific point (jagah, waqt, position, ya event) ko refer karta hai.

### 1. Specific Place (Mukhssus jagah par)
* Jab kisi exact jagah ki baat ho – building, corner, gate, etc.

1. He is at the door.
(Woh darwazay par hai)

2. I’m at home.
(Main ghar par hoon)

3. She is at school.
(Woh school par hai)


###  2. Specific Time (Mukhssus waqt par)
* Kisi exact waqt ya time point ke liye.

1. The class starts at 9 AM.
(Class 9 bajay shuru hoti hai)

2. I’ll call you at 5 o’clock.
(Main tumhe 5 bajay call karunga)


### 3. Events / Occasions (Mouqa ya event par)
* Kisi event ya function ke waqt.

1. I met him at the party.
(Main usse party par mila)

2. They were at the wedding.
(Woh shaadi par thay)


### 4. Email / Digital Address (Contact Point)
* Jab kisi email ya online address ka zikr ho.

1. Contact me at info@example.com
(Mujh se is email par raabta karein)

2. The website is at www.example.com
(Website is address par hai)

### 5. Expressions of State or Action (Haalaat batane ke liye)
* Kisi shakhs ya cheez ki haalat ya activity batane ke liye.

1. He is at work.
(Woh kaam par hai)

2. She laughed at me.
(Usne mujh par hansi)

3. They are at war.
(Woh jang mein hain)


## In
"In" ka matlab hota hai "andar" — kisi cheez ke andar hone ka izhar karta hai (enclosed ya limited space).

### 1. Enclosed Space (Band ya confined jagah ke andar)
Jab koi cheez kisi aur cheez ke andar ho (boundary ke andar ho).

1. The keys are in the drawer.
(Chaabiyan drawer mein hain)

2. He is in the room.
(Woh kamray mein hai)

3. Water is in the bottle.
(Pani bottle mein hai)


### 2. Countries / Cities / Areas (Geographical jagah ke andar)
* Kisi mulk, shehar ya ilaqay ke andar rehna.

1. I live in Pakistan.
(Main Pakistan mein rehta hoon)

2. She works in Lahore.
(Woh Lahore mein kaam karti hai)

3. He was born in the village.
(Woh gaon mein paida hua tha)


### 3. Time Period (Waqt ke andar)
* Kisi waqt ya duration ke andar.

1. I will return in 10 minutes.
(Main 10 minute mein wapas aaoon ga)

2. She was born in 2000.
(Woh 2000 mein paida hui thi)

3. He will complete it in a week.
(Woh isay ek hafta mein complete karega)


### 4. Clothing / Condition / Form (Kis halat ya libaas mein)
* Kis libaas ya surat mein ho.

1. He came in a black suit.
(Woh black suit mein aaya)

2. The man is in pain.
(Woh aadmi dard mein hai)

3. She is in trouble.
(Woh mushkil mein hai)


### 5. Languages, Books, Media, etc.
* Kis language ya medium mein.

1. The movie is in Urdu.
(Movie Urdu mein hai)

2. The book is in English.
(Kitaab English mein hai)

3. Write the letter in capital letters.
(Letter capital letters mein likho)
