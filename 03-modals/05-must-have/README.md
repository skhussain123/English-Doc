


### Must Have
"Must have" ek modal perfect structure hai jo kisi kaam ke past mein zaroor hone ka yaqeen dikhata hai. Jab aapko kisi kaam ke ho jane ka strong certainty ho lekin aap us waqt present mein nahi the, tab aap "must have + past participle" ka use karte ho.

* He must have left already.
وہ ضرور جا چکا ہوگا۔

* She must have forgotten your birthday.
اسے ضرور تمہاری سالگرہ یاد نہیں رہی ہوگی۔

* You must have seen the news.
تم نے ضرور خبریں دیکھی ہوں گی۔

* They must have reached home by now.
وہ اب تک ضرور گھر پہنچ چکے ہوں گے۔



####  Compare with "Might Have" vs "Must Have":
| Sentence                | Certainty Level       | Urdu                 |
| ----------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------- |
| He **might have left**. | 50% (ho sakta hai)    | وہ شاید جا چکا ہو۔   |
| He **must have left**.  | 90–100% (pura yaqeen) | وہ ضرور جا چکا ہوگا۔ |


#### Common Situations for "Must Have":
| Situation                  | Example                                | Urdu                               |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Logical conclusion         | You **must have worked hard**.         | تم نے ضرور محنت کی ہوگی۔           |
| Guessing based on evidence | She **must have heard** the noise.     | اس نے ضرور آواز سنی ہوگی۔          |
| Missed something           | I **must have left** my phone at home. | میں نے ضرور فون گھر چھوڑ دیا ہوگا۔ |
