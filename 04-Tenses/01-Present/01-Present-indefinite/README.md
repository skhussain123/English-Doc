### ✅ Present Indefinite Tense (Present Simple)

**تعریف:**  
Present Indefinite Tense woh tense hai jo **aadat (habit)**, **daily routine**, **universal truths**, aur **facts** ko ظاہر کرتا ہے۔

---

### 🔹 استعمال (Usage):

| Category           | Description                                      | Example (English)              | Example (Urdu)                      |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 1. **Habit**        | Aadat jo bar bar hoti hai                      | He drinks tea every morning.   | وہ ہر صبح چائے پیتا ہے۔              |
| 2. **Routine**      | Rozana ka schedule ya kaam                      | I go to school at 8 a.m.       | میں صبح 8 بجے اسکول جاتا ہوں۔         |
| 3. **Universal Truth** | Har waqt sahi hone wali baat                | The sun rises in the east.     | سورج مشرق سے نکلتا ہے۔                |
| 4. **Facts**        | Haqeeqat par mabni baat                        | Water boils at 100°C.          | پانی 100 ڈگری پر اُبلتا ہے۔          |

---

### 🔹 Helping Verbs:
- **Do** → I, we, you, they  
- **Does** → he, she, it, singular noun

---

### 🔹 Sentence Structures:

| Type          | Structure                                  | Example                        |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Affirmative   | Subject + Verb (s/es) + Object              | She plays tennis.               |
| Negative      | Subject + do/does + not + Verb (base form) | He does not like tea.           |
| Interrogative | Do/Does + Subject + Verb (base form)?      | Do you speak English?           |

---

### ✅ Extra Tip:
- **I, You, We, They** ke saath **do** aur verb ki base form lagti hai.  
- **He, She, It** ke saath **does** lagta hai aur verb me **s/es** lagta hai (affirmative me only).

---

### 🔹 Sample Sentences:

#### ✅ Affirmative:
- He speaks English.
- I live in Karachi.
- They go to the mosque.

#### ❌ Negative:
- He does not speak English.
- I do not live in Lahore.
- They do not go to the market.

#### ❓ Interrogative:
- Does he speak English?
- Do you like pizza?
- Do they play cricket?
