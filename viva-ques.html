Six Kalimas

The 6 Kalimas

1. Introduction

Sir mera name hussain ha or mera taluk distric malir sa ha. Maine 2021 mein Intermediate complete kiya. Iske baad maine Aptech se 3 saal ka Diploma software engineering me complete kiya. Mere father ka naam Faheem-ud-Din hai. jo pichle 35 saalon se Sindh District Police mein Constable ke taur par apne faraiz anjaam de rahe hain. or Unhi se inspire hokr main Sindh Prison Police join karna chahta hoon, taake main apne mulk, awam, department aur apne senior officers ki imaandari aur lagan ke saath khidmat kar sakoon.


2. Aap Sindh Jail Police mein kyun shamil hona chahte hain

Sir Main Sindh Jail Police mein shamil hokr apne mulk ki khidmat karna chahta hoon. or jail mein aman-o-amaan aur nazm-o-zabt barqarar rakhne mein apna kirdar ada karna chahta hoon.


3. Hum Aap Ko Kyun Muntakhib Karein?

Sir, main aik mehnati, nazm-o-zabt ka paband aur jismani tor par fit shakhs hoon. Main dabao mein bhi kaam kar sakta hoon. Main mehkame ki taraf se di gayi kisi bhi qisam ki duty anjaam dene ke liye tayyar hoon. Agar mujhe muntakhib kiya gaya to main apne faraiz imaandari aur zimmedari ke saath anjaam doon ga.

4. Jail Police Ahalkaar Ke Faraiz Kya Hain?

1. Qaidioun ki hifazat aur nigrani karna aur jail ki security ko yaqini banana.
2. Jail mein aman-o-amaan aur nazm-o-zabt barqarar rakhna.
3. Qaidioun ko faraar hone se rokna
4. Jail mein larai jhagray aur ghair-qanooni sargarmiyon ko rokna. 5. Apne senior afsaraan ke ahkamaat par amal karna.

5. ASI Aur Constable Mein Kya Farq Hai?

1. ASI ka rank Constable se bara hota hai.ASI ki zimmedariyon mein nigrani aur intizami umoor shamil hote hain. or zaroorat ke mutabiq Constables ko duty ki hidayaat deta hai
2. Constable ki zimmedariyon mein security, gasht aur rozmarra ki duty shamil hoti hai.

6. Aap Ki Sab Se Bari Khoobiyan Kya Hain?

sir meri iman dar , nazm-o-zabt, sabr, or dabao mein kaam karne jasi qualities mojood ha. Main mehkame ki taraf se di gayi kisi bhi qisam ki duty anjaam dene ke liye tayyar hoon

7. Aap Ki Sab Se Bari Khamiyah Kya Hain

Sir, main kabhi kabhi apne kaam mein itni tawajjo deta hoon ke time ka khayal nahi rehta, lekin ab main kaam ke saath waqt ki pabandi ka bhi khayal rakhta hoon.

8. Kya Aap Pressure Mein Kaam Kar Sakte Hain?

Ji Sir, main pressure mein bhi kaam kar sakta hoon. Mushkil halaat mein ghabrane ke bajaye pur-sukoon rehty howy kam krta hon. Zaroorat parne par senior afsaraan se rehnumai leta hoon.

9. Agar Koi Senior Officer Aapko Ghair-Qanooni Hukam De To Aap Kya Karenge?

Sir, main apne senior afsaraan ka ehtram karta hoon aur respectfuly me ghair-qanooni kam se mana krdunga. aur department ke qawaid-o-zawabit ke mutabik Senior Officer ko report karunga.

11. Duty Ke Doran Agar Aap Se Koi Ghalti Ho Jaye To Aap Kya Karenge?

Sir, main apni ghalti tasleem karunga, foran apne senior officer ko ittila dunga aur usay durust karunga, taake ainda aisi ghalti dobara na ho.

13. Agar Koi Ba-Asar Shakhs Aap Se Qanoon Tornay Ko Kahe To Aap Kya Karenge?

Sir, main respectfuly me ghair-qanooni kam se mana krdunga.Agar woh shakhs phir bhi israr kare to main apne senior officer ko ittila dunga.

14. Kya Aap Mustaqbil Mein Jail Police Se Istifa De Kar District Police Mein Shamil Ho Jayenge?

Sir Agar mujhe is mehkame mein khidmat ka mauqa mila to main ise apna paka career samajh kar imaandari aur mehnat se apne faraiz anjaam dunga. Mera maqsad isi mehkame mein behtar karkardagi dikhana aur taraqqi karna hai.

15. Aap Ke Walid District Police Mein Hain, To Aap Jail Police Mein Kyun Aana Chahte Hain?

Sir, Jail Police ka kaam mujhe alag aur aham lagta hai, kyun ke yahan qanoon par amal karwane ke saath qaidioun ki islaah bhi hoti hai. isi liye Main is shobay mein nazm-o-zabt, or imaandari se apny faraiz anjam dena chahta hoon.

16. Aap Ne Is Se Pehle Kisi Sarkari Naukri Ke Liye Apply Kyun Nahi Kiya?

Sir, is se pehle main apni taleem mukammal karne aur apni salahiyaton ko behtar banane par tawajjo de raha tha. Main chahta tha ke pehle apni taleem mukammal karoon aur phir kisi achhi sarkari naukri ke liye koshish karoon. Ab main apni taleem mukammal kar chuka hoon aur sarkari naukri ke liye khud ko tayyar samajhta hoon.

17. Agar Duty Ke Doran Koi Aap Ke Saath Bad-Tameezi Kare To Aap Kya Karenge?

Sir, main pur-sukoon rahunga aur professional rawayya ikhtiyar karunga. Main jazbati ho kar jawab nahi dunga. Main qanoon aur mehkame ke tareeqa-e-kaar ke mutabiq surat-e-haal ko hal karne ki koshish karunga.

18. Diploma kyun kia software engineering me

Sir, maine Diploma apni taleem aur salahiyaton ko behtar banane ke liye kiya hai. Lekin meri dilchaspi sirf computer ke shobay tak mehdood nahi hai. Main Prison Department join karna chahta hoon. Agar mujhe mauqa mila to main apni computer skills ko bhi department ke liye kam karunga aur apne department ki behtari ke liye mehnat karunga.

19. What Your Hobby

Mera shoq running aur physical exercise hai. Mujhe apne aap ko fit aur healthy rakhna pasand hai. Main regular running practice karta hoon, kyun ke is se meri physical fitness, stamina aur discipline behtar hota hai.
