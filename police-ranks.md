## 2. Sindh Police Responsibilities

General duties and reporting relationships by rank.

### 01. Police Constable (PC) — BPS-07
**Responsibilities:** Performs duties at checkpoints, roads, police stations and patrols. Assists with receiving crime reports, basic investigations, public dealing, traffic control and escorts.
**Reports to:** Head Constable (HC) and relevant supervisory officers.

### 02. Head Constable (HC) — BPS-09
**Responsibilities:** Supervises constables, assigns duties, assists with minor investigations and helps manage day-to-day police station work.
**Reports to:** ASI or Sub-Inspector, according to the assignment.

### 03. Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) — BPS-11
**Responsibilities:** Assists in investigations and FIR-related procedures, leads patrol teams when assigned and handles matters within authorized powers.
**Reports to:** Sub-Inspector (SI), Inspector or other designated officer.

### 04. Sub-Inspector (SI) — BPS-14
**Responsibilities:** Investigates cases when assigned, performs police station operational duties and may serve as Station House Officer (SHO), subject to appointment.
**Reports to:** Inspector or designated DSP/ASP supervisory officer.

### 05. Inspector — BPS-16
**Responsibilities:** May serve as SHO, supervise investigations, manage staff and help maintain law and order. Handles cases according to assigned authority.
**Reports to:** Relevant DSP/ASP or other designated senior officer.

### 06. Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) — BPS-17
**Responsibilities:** Generally a Police Service of Pakistan (PSP) officer. May supervise a police sub-division, operations, investigations and training.
**Reports to:** Designated SP or other senior officer.

### 07. Deputy Superintendent of Police (DSP) — BPS-17
**Responsibilities:** May supervise a police sub-division (SDPO), police stations, law-and-order arrangements and investigation monitoring.
**Reports to:** Designated SP or other senior officer.

### 08. Superintendent of Police (SP) — BPS-18
**Responsibilities:** Manages an assigned district, division or specialist unit, including administration, crime control and operational planning.
**Reports to:** SSP or another designated senior officer, depending on the posting.

### 09. Senior Superintendent of Police (SSP) — BPS-19
**Responsibilities:** May lead a district or specialized unit and supervise SPs and other officers according to the command structure.
**Reports to:** Relevant DIG or designated senior officer.

### 10. Deputy Inspector General (DIG) — BPS-20
**Responsibilities:** May oversee a range, region, zone or specialist wing, supervise districts and monitor implementation of departmental policies.
**Reports to:** Relevant Additional IG or designated senior officer.

### 11. Additional Inspector General (Addl. IG) — BPS-21
**Responsibilities:** May head major ranges or specialist wings, supervise DIGs and oversee high-level operational or administrative matters.
**Reports to:** Inspector General of Police (IGP), according to the assigned portfolio.

### 12. Inspector General of Police (IGP) — BPS-22
**Responsibilities:** Heads the provincial police force, oversees command and administration, sets priorities and coordinates with the provincial government.
**Reports to:** Relevant provincial government authorities under the applicable administrative framework.

*Note: Actual reporting lines, duties and appointments depend on official postings, departmental rules and the specific unit. Rank order does not always equal direct supervisory responsibility.*

## 4. Prison Department Responsibilities

General duties and supervisory relationships.

### 01. Prison Constable (PC) — BPS-05
**Responsibilities:** Maintains prison security, monitors prisoners, guards gates and barracks, assists with prisoner counts and escorts.
**Reports to:** Senior Prison Constable, Head Constable or assigned supervisor.

### 02. Senior Prison Constable (Sr. PC) — BPS-05
**Responsibilities:** Supports daily security work, helps coordinate constables and performs team supervision when assigned.
**Reports to:** Head Constable or designated supervisor.

### 03. Head Constable Prison (HC) — BPS-07
**Responsibilities:** Supervises constables, helps manage shift duties, checks barracks and maintains discipline and security procedures.
**Reports to:** ASI or designated senior officer.

### 04. Assistant Sub-Inspector Prison (ASI) — BPS-09
**Responsibilities:** May supervise a ward or section, oversee prisoner counts and security rounds, and monitor subordinate staff.
**Reports to:** Sub-Inspector or designated supervisor.

### 05. Sub-Inspector (SI) — BPS-14
**Responsibilities:** May supervise a major section or block, coordinate security operations, manage staff and control prisoner movement.
**Reports to:** Assistant Superintendent or assigned senior officer.

### 06. Assistant Superintendent — BPS-16
**Responsibilities:** Assists with daily prison administration, security, discipline, prisoner records and staff supervision. May manage assigned wings.
**Reports to:** Deputy Superintendent or designated senior officer.

### 07. Deputy Superintendent — BPS-17
**Responsibilities:** Assists the Superintendent with prison operations, security, discipline, prisoner welfare and staff management.
**Reports to:** Superintendent Prison or designated senior officer.

### 08. Superintendent Prison — BPS-18
**Responsibilities:** Generally leads an assigned jail, overseeing security, administration, prisoner safety, discipline, welfare and daily management.
**Reports to:** Relevant senior prison authority under the applicable structure.

### 09. Senior Superintendent Prison — BPS-19
**Responsibilities:** May manage a major prison or perform senior administrative and inspection duties, according to the assigned post.
**Reports to:** Relevant DIG or designated senior authority.

### 10. Deputy Inspector General of Prisons (DIG) — BPS-20
**Responsibilities:** May supervise a region or zone, inspect prisons, monitor policy implementation and coordinate responses to major issues.
**Reports to:** Inspector General of Prisons or designated senior authority.

### 11. Inspector General of Prisons (IG) — BPS-21
**Responsibilities:** Heads the provincial prisons department, oversees administration and security standards, coordinates with government authorities and supports prison reforms and prisoner welfare.
**Reports to:** Relevant Sindh Government authorities under the applicable administrative framework.

## Armed Forces of Pakistan

Pakistan Army zameeni sarhadon ki hifazat aur mulk ke difa ki zimmedar hai. Pakistan Navy (aur Pakistan Marines) samandri sarhadon aur bandargahon ki hifazat karti hai, jabke Pakistan Air Force (PAF) fazai hudood ki hifazat aur hawai difa ka kaam anjam deti hai.

## Intelligence Agencies

ISI bairooni aur andaroni khufia maloomat aur qaumi salamti ke liye kaam karti hai. IB mulk ke andar siyasi aur dehshatgardi se mutaliq intelligence jama karti hai, jabke MI fauj ke liye intelligence aur counter-intelligence ki zimmedar hai. NACTA dehshatgardi ke khilaf kaam karne wale idaron ke darmiyan coordination aur policy banane ka kaam karti hai.

## Paramilitary / Semi-Military Forces

Pakistan Rangers (Sindh aur Punjab) sarhad ki hifazat aur Karachi mein amn-o-aman qaim rakhti hai, jabke Frontier Corps (FC) Afghan aur Iran border ki nigrani karti hai aur KP aur Balochistan mein amn ke liye kaam karti hai. Pakistan Coast Guards Sindh ke sahil par smuggling aur gair qanooni aamad-o-raft ki roktham karti hai. Gilgit-Baltistan Scouts Gilgit-Baltistan ke pahari ilaqon aur sarhad ki hifazat karti hai. Maritime Security Agency (MSA) Pakistan ke samandri ilaqe mein nigrani karti hai aur ghair qanooni machhli shikar aur smuggling ki roktham karti hai. Federal Constabulary wafaq ke ilaqon mein qanoon nafiz karti hai aur amn-o-aman mein madad deti hai.

## Federal Law Enforcement

FIA wafaqi jurm ki tehqeeqat karti hai, jaise cyber crime, human trafficking aur immigration fraud. NAB corruption aur ikhtiyarat ke na-jaiz istemal ki tehqeeqat karta hai, jabke ANF manshiyat (drugs) ki smuggling aur istemal ki roktham karti hai. ASF airports ki hifazat karti hai aur Pakistan Customs import-export par duty wasooli aur smuggling ki roktham ka kaam karta hai. NHMP motorways aur national highways par traffic aur hifazat sanbhalti hai, jabke ICT Police Islamabad mein amn-o-aman aur jurm ki roktham karti hai.

## Provincial Police aur Units

Sindh Police, Punjab Police, KP Police aur Balochistan Police apne apne soobay mein amn-o-aman aur jurm ki roktham karti hain. CTD dehshatgardi ki tehqeeqat aur roktham karta hai, jabke Special Branch soobay mein khufia maloomat aur siyasi nigrani ka kaam karti hai. Elite Police / SSU khusoosi training ke saath VIP hifazat aur khatarnak operations anjam deti hai. Railway Police railway stations aur trains mein hifazat karti hai, aur Anti-Corruption Establishment sarkari mulazimeen ki corruption ki tehqeeqat karta hai.
