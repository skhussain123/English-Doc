## 2. Present Negative
#### [Sub + do/does + not + V1 + obj]

* Singular (He, She, It, Name) → does not (doesn’t) + base verb
* Plural (I, We, You, They) → do not (don’t) + base verb

| Type               | Subject    | Negative Form             | Example Sentence                       |
| ------------------ | ---------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Singular           | He         | does not (doesn’t) + verb | He **doesn't play** football.          |
| Singular           | She        | does not (doesn’t) + verb | She **doesn't like** tea.              |
| Singular           | It         | does not (doesn’t) + verb | It **doesn't work** properly.          |
| Singular           | Ali (Name) | does not (doesn’t) + verb | Ali **doesn't go** to school.          |
| Singular (special) | I          | do not (don’t) + verb     | I **don’t eat** meat.                  |
| Plural             | We         | do not (don’t) + verb     | We **don’t watch** TV.                 |
| Plural             | They       | do not (don’t) + verb     | They **don’t study** at night.         |
| Plural/Singular    | You        | do not (don’t) + verb     | You **don’t understand** the question. |
| Plural             | My friends | do not (don’t) + verb     | My friends **don’t call** me often.    |
