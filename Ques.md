# Islamabad Police Interview Questions & Answers

## 1. Introduction (Apna Taaruf)

*(Is jawab ka sawal image mein nahi tha, shayad "Tell me about yourself / Introduce yourself" hai.)*

"My name is ______. I am from Islamabad. I have completed my ______. I am a hardworking, disciplined and responsible person. I want to join Islamabad Police because I want to serve the people, maintain law and order, and contribute positively to society."

## 2. Why do you want to join Islamabad Police?

"I want to join Islamabad Police because it is a respected department and provides an opportunity to serve the people. I want to work with honesty, discipline and dedication and help maintain peace and security."

## 3. Why should we select you?

"Sir, I am hardworking, disciplined and physically fit. I can work under pressure and I am willing to follow lawful orders. If selected, I will perform my duties honestly and responsibly."

## 4. What are the duties of a police officer?

"The main duties are to maintain law and order, protect life and property, prevent and investigate crime, assist citizens, enforce the law and maintain public safety."

## 5. What is the difference between ASI and Constable?

"A Constable performs basic police duties and assists senior officers in maintaining law and order. An ASI has greater supervisory and investigative responsibilities and may supervise police personnel and handle assigned cases according to law."

## 6. What is your greatest strength?

"My greatest strengths are discipline, honesty, patience and my ability to work under pressure."

## 7. What is your weakness?

"Sometimes I focus too much on completing my work perfectly, but I am learning to manage my time more efficiently."

**Tip:** Weakness mein aisi cheez na batana jo police duty ke liye serious problem lage.

## 8. Can you work under pressure?

"Yes, sir. I remain calm under pressure, focus on the situation and try to make a responsible decision according to the law and instructions of my senior officers."

## 9. If a senior gives you an illegal order, what will you do?

"Sir, I will respectfully explain my concern and follow the law and departmental rules. I will not knowingly perform an illegal act."

## 10. If an influential person asks you to break the law?

"I will politely refuse and perform my duty according to the law. For a police officer, the law should be above personal influence."

## 11. If someone abuses you while you are on duty?

"I will remain calm and professional. I will not respond emotionally. I will try to resolve the situation according to law and departmental procedure."

## 12. What is discipline?

"Discipline means following lawful rules, maintaining proper conduct and performing one's duty responsibly and on time."

## 13. What does public service mean to you?

"Public service means helping citizens, protecting their rights and safety, and performing one's responsibilities honestly without discrimination."

## 14. What will you do if you make a mistake on duty?

"I will accept my mistake, immediately inform my senior officer when required, correct it according to procedure and learn from it so that it does not happen again."

## 15. What is the most important quality of a police officer?

"In my opinion, integrity is one of the most important qualities, along with discipline, courage, patience and respect for the law."
